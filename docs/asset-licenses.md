## Attribution

- CC BY assets are credited on `/credits` (title, author, source link, licence link, what was changed), linked from the footer and from `/building`. The list lives in `src/content/credits.ts`; adding a CC BY asset means adding its entry in the same commit.
- Not used: "city at night low poly skyscrapers" (Sketchfab Standard licence, which restricts redistribution of the raw file; the sky is generated in code instead) and "Sing Sing Prison" (222 MB scan of a real prison, unsuitable for the site).
- Also not used: "artcollection room with office" (an indoor pool and spa; showing it would suggest an amenity the branches do not have), and "free loft 18", "loft 16 interior", "the interior 14" and "realistic road" (Sketchfab Standard licence, same restriction as above).
- The background music is CC0. Commercial chart or TikTok songs are not used: TikTok's licence covers use inside TikTok only, not a business website.
- Apache-2.0, OFL and ISC need the license text kept with the distributed files, not on-screen credit. The texts ship inside the npm packages in `package-lock.json`.
- Open: written confirmation from the owner for `tlv-rothschild-hot-desk.webp` should be saved as `docs/licenses/owner-flagship-photo.pdf` (or an email export) before launch.

## Rules

- Renders must say "הדמיה" visibly and end their alt text with `(הדמיה)`; `src/content/seed-data.test.ts` enforces this for seed content.
- Replace a render with a real photo of the same room as soon as one exists, and drop the marker from its alt text.
- No hotlinked stock: images are served from `public/` or Sanity's CDN only (see `img-src` in `src/lib/security/csp.ts`).
